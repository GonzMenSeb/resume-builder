JOB POSTING: Senior Full Stack Engineer

Company: TechCorp Innovation Labs
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid - 3 days on-site)
Employment Type: Full-Time
Experience Level: Senior (5+ years)
Salary Range: $180,000 - $240,000 + equity

---

ABOUT THE ROLE

We're looking for a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join our platform team and help us build the next generation of our infrastructure serving millions of users worldwide. You'll work with a talented team of engineers on both frontend and backend systems, taking ownership of critical features and mentoring junior engineers.

This is a high-impact role where you'll have the opportunity to make architectural decisions, drive technical excellence, and shape the future of our platform. You'll own projects end-to-end, from design to production deployment.

---

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Design and implement scalable backend services and APIs using modern Node.js frameworks
- Build responsive, high-performance user interfaces using React and TypeScript
- Lead architectural discussions and propose solutions for complex technical challenges
- Mentor junior and mid-level engineers through code reviews, pair programming, and technical guidance
- Collaborate with product managers, designers, and other engineers to deliver features that customers love
- Participate in code reviews, design reviews, and technical discussions to maintain code quality
- Contribute to improving developer experience, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure
- Take ownership of production systems and participate in on-call rotation
- Identify and drive improvements to system performance, scalability, and reliability

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong expertise in modern JavaScript/TypeScript and at least one backend language (Node.js preferred)
- Proven experience building and maintaining scalable web applications at scale
- Deep knowledge of React or similar modern frontend frameworks
- Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL) and noSQL databases (MongoDB, Redis)
- Solid understanding of system design, scalability, and distributed systems concepts
- Experience with containerization and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes) or willingness to learn
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent professional experience
- Strong communication skills and ability to work effectively in a collaborative environment
- Commitment to writing clean, testable, maintainable code
- Experience with Git and modern development workflows
